# PalaRivals Watch Development Archive

## Version 0.8.0 — Ability Draft Evolution & Balance Changes
Date: August 20, 2026
Status: Current Build

### Balance Changes
- Hulk: After Traits and Merging was added, we felt his power was underwhelming so we updated his ability
> Worldbreaker: Power Gain 2 to 4, 
- Spiderman: we felt his power was just inferior to his counter part Tracer especially for someone that costs more, so we gave his ability a bit of update
> Spider-Sense: Removed his bonus on first strike
> New Effect: 3 bonus damage to enemies at or below 35% health.
- Thor: He didnt really have a place in the game, so we are testing out increasing his power by buffing his crit rate
> God Of Thunder: Crit rate 0.08 to 0.18
- Tracer: She is the definitive strongest tier 1 character by a long shot with her new ability with the overwatch trait so we are toning down her monervabilty.
> Blink Recall: Dodge Chance 0.25 to 0.18
- Moji: He is very weak compared to the other tier 1 units so we have increased his execution capabilities to hopefully bring him up closer to the other tier 1 units
> Familar Feast: Execution Threshold 0.4 to 0.5, Execution Bonus Damage 4 to 8
- Raum: With the changes with the traits and merging he kinda fell behind in power so we wanted to make his tier 4 presence more threatening.
> Soul Armor: Lifesteal 0.20 to 0.25
- Seris: Her survivablity was pretty low so we are trying to increase that slightly
> Soul Collector: Health after kill 2 to 3

### Ability Draft
- Added six-slot hero repositioning and occupied-slot swapping.
- Matching heroes can now merge from level 1 through level 4.
- Merged heroes gain additional power and health.
- Heroes with different equipped abilities now create a **fused loadout** that keeps every effect active.
- AI opponents can create leveled heroes and fused ability combinations.
- Added complete hover, focus, and mobile-tap information panels for every unit.

### Interface
- Added clearer merge targets, level indicators, progression displays, and fusion styling.
- Improved the Ability Draft layout on desktop and mobile screens.

> Ability Draft remains an experimental Arcade mode and will continue receiving balance changes.

## Version 0.7.0 — Arcade Protocol
Date: August 20, 2026
Status: Major Feature

### New Mode
- Added the Arcade menu to the main screen.
- Introduced **Ability Draft**, where any offered ability can be installed on any recruited hero.
- Added a dedicated hero shop, ability market, build timer, and animated combat presentation.

### Game Systems
- Added enemy draft rivals with progressively stronger teams.
- Added round victories, defeats, integrity damage, credits, and run completion.
